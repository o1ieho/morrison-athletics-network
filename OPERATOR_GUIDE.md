# Game-day operator guide

One page for whoever is running stats. Practise once in demo mode before your first real game.

## Before tip-off

1. Charge the laptop or tablet. Landscape works best.
2. Sign in at **/login**, then open **/operator** and pick the game.
3. Check the top right says **Connected** and **All saved**.
4. Keep a paper scoresheet going as a backup for the first few games.

## During the game

| To do this | Tap |
|---|---|
| Start the game | **Start game (Q1)** |
| Start or stop the clock | **Start / Stop** (or press **Space**) |
| Fix the clock | Tap the clock numbers, type e.g. `4:32`, then **Set** |
| Log a Broncos stat | Player, then stat. The other order works too: stat, then player. |
| Log a shot with its location | Player, then tap the court, then **Made** or **Missed** (2 or 3 is detected automatically) |
| Credit an assist | After a made shot, tap the passer's number (or **No assist**) |
| Offensive rebound after a miss | Tap the rebounder's number (or **Not ours**) |
| Team rebound or turnover | **Team**, then the stat |
| Opponent scores, fouls, timeouts | The bar along the bottom (team totals only, no players) |
| Undo a mistake | **Undo last** (or **Ctrl+Z**), or the ✕ next to any play |
| Bring back an undone play | The ↺ next to it |
| End a quarter | **End Q1** (etc.), then **Start Q2** when play resumes |
| Finish | After Q4 ends: **Finish game** |

Scores come from the plays you log, so to fix a score, fix the play.

## If the Wi-Fi drops

Keep logging. Plays wait on the device (**"3 sending…"**) and send themselves when the connection comes back, in the right order and with the right game clock. Don't close the tab while plays are waiting; the browser will warn you. If the red **failed** badge appears at the top, tap it to retry.

## Player fouls

A player's button turns yellow at 4 fouls and shows **OUT** at 5. **Bonus** under a team means that team shoots free throws on the other side's fouls.

## After the game

Check the box score on the public page (**Open public page ↗**). If a stat is wrong, reopen the game from **/operator → Recent** and remove or restore plays. Scores and season stats update automatically.
